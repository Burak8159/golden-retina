## Additional catalog findings and dispositions

- **BMRD:** 1,786-image Bangladesh multi-disease fundus dataset, present in the candidate inventory. The staged release still lacks a verified per-file disease mapping for most records, so it is not eligible for disease-specific fitting until labels can be reconciled with the official sidecar.
- **Mendeley 22,454-image retinal compilation:** screened, but mixed-source lineage and ambiguous filenames prevent safe label assignment or a defensible held-out-source test.
- **AMDNet23:** a derived compilation from HRF, ODIR, and RFMiD. It is not counted as independent evidence from those parent datasets and should not be used as a supposedly new external cohort.
- **BRSET and mBRSET:** high-value, patient-linked Brazilian cohorts, but PhysioNet access is credentialed and agreement-bound. They are not downloaded unless the user's dataset-specific account access is active and the applicable agreement is completed.
- **HYAMD:** the PhysioNet project page marks the dataset Restricted Access. The present account grant and project-specific agreement are not verified, so it is not staged.
- **AIROGS and other noncommercial or controlled glaucoma cohorts:** retained as future candidates only after checking the current primary-source terms and actual access route; a catalog label alone is not permission or an access grant.
- **OCT, angiography, slit-lamp, corneal topography, visual-field, and electrophysiology cohorts:** excluded from fundus-camera disease claims when the target product does not acquire that modality. They can only support a separate modality-specific model.

### Cloud-only metadata audit for the structural release

The three small Figshare metadata spreadsheets were fetched to the L4 VM only (no fundus images were fetched from this release). The source-image mapping has 1,948 rows and 19 disease-folder categories. Its reported counts are source mappings, **not** counts of images actually included in the CC BY archive. Exact categories with source images explicitly not redistributed include Glaucoma 965 (REFUGE2 800, BEH 165), ODIR-5K cataract 259, and TJDR DR 168. Four disease archive ZIP central directories were checked through HTTP range requests; all entries were PNG masks, not fundus photographs. A source mapping or mask does not grant access to its linked original photograph. An aggregate-only audit record is stored under `results/multidisease_segmentation_metadata_20261122/` on the VM; row-level source filenames remain cloud-local.

### AMDNet23 disposition

[AMDNet23](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/yj35kjgrv3/1) is an official Mendeley Data CC BY 4.0 record containing 2,000 enhanced fundus images, including 400 AMD images. The record names six parent sources, including ODIR, RFMiD, HRF and ARIA. This makes it a possible training augmentation source, but not a new independent AMD cohort. It was not staged yet because parent-source/image-level overlap must be audited against the current training pool; its four balanced classes also do not reflect clinical prevalence.

## Training/evaluation priorities for this campaign

1. Complete CLARUS700's validation-only encoder comparison and one frozen official patient-level test pass. Report disease-level AUROC, average precision, thresholds, sensitivity, specificity, errors, and patient-bootstrap intervals; disclose small positive support and the one-site/device boundary.
2. Train and evaluate the already-staged ACRIMA glaucoma task with exact-hash-grouped image splits; no patient-independence claim.
3. Complete the FIVES archive/hash audit. Train a four-class AMD/DR/glaucoma/normal model on the official FIVES training partition, carve validation only from that partition, remove known exact overlaps from the official image-level test, and score the remaining test once. Patient leakage remains unresolved.
4. Extract and verify MuReD on the L4, then run its 20-label ConvNeXt training using only its supplied train/validation split. This is development-only because patient identifiers are absent and parent-source overlap with ARIA/STARE/RFMiD is expected.
5. Train the UWF tumor six-class model only after its archive audit; use a deterministic exact-duplicate-group split and report image-level support and errors, not patient-level performance.
6. Do not count access-gated datasets as trained. Record the actual access gate and proceed with already-authorized, auditable sources.

## Evidence boundaries

The word “accuracy” is not interchangeable across these tasks. Multiclass accuracy, per-disease sensitivity/specificity, AUROC, average precision, and false-negative counts answer different questions. Validation metrics are for model selection. Only the prespecified, untouched test partition is a test result. Image-level or duplicate-grouped scores without patient IDs are exploratory. None of the current public-dataset runs alone establishes clinical utility, device performance in the intended workflow, or safe autonomous diagnosis.
