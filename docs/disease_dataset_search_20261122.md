# Retinal disease dataset search and execution queue — 2026-11-22

## Scope

This is a search-and-use inventory for diseases that can plausibly be assessed from color fundus or ultra-widefield fundus photographs. It is not a claim that one model detects every eye disease. Corneal/topographic disease, anterior-segment disease, OCT-only macular conditions, fluorescein-angiography-only findings, and functional visual-field outcomes are not inferable as standalone diagnoses from a fundus photograph and are excluded from the fundus classifier queue.

## Disease-by-disease scope for fundus camera input

- **Supported for focused fundus development when labels and sample counts permit:** DR severity and referable proxies; AMD; glaucoma or glaucoma-suspect screening; retinal vein occlusion; retinal detachment; pathological myopia; uveitis; selected intraocular tumors; image quality/gradability only where quality labels are available.
- **Potentially assessable but currently weakly supported:** diabetic macular edema risk from fundus grades; hypertensive retinal changes; central serous chorioretinopathy, epiretinal membrane, retinal artery occlusion, optic neuropathy and uncommon inflammatory/degenerative findings. Keep each separate and require adequate adjudicated fundus labels.
- **Excluded from a fundus-only disease claim:** keratoconus (requires corneal shape/topography), keratitis and other anterior-segment disorders without an anterior camera, OCT-defined macular edema/ERM when diagnosis depends on OCT, angiography-only vascular leakage, visual-field-only functional damage, and systemic disease claims not directly labeled against a fundus reference standard.

1. Finish FDR26's prespecified patient-grouped encoder comparison, validation-only threshold choice, and one-shot test. Do not tune on the test.
2. Complete the newly staged PAPILA patient-grouped glaucoma/suspect experiment if the exact-source scan and split audit retain usable class support.
3. Use remaining L4 time on the queued UWF tumor task only if the existing audit supports a clean exact-hash-group experiment; report the absence of patient IDs prominently.
4. Stage and audit IDRiD on the L4 only if enough closeout time remains. Do not start it if it would compromise safe artifact pull, verification, or shutdown.
5. Keep source-specific metrics separate. No pooled “all eye disease” accuracy; no clinical diagnosis or deployment claim from these retrospective benchmark runs.

### New patient-grouped outcomes

PAPILA v2 was completed with a patient-disjoint 294/98/96-eye split (147/49/48 patients) and zero exact-hash matches against five scanned glaucoma/fundus roots. ResNet-50 was selected by validation loss. Its one-shot internal test (96 eyes, 48 patients) yielded three-class accuracy 69.79%, balanced accuracy 45.54%, macro-F1 46.29% and macro-AUROC 0.787. For the prespecified validation-selected glaucoma-or-suspect screen, test sensitivity was 36.67% (19/30 positive eyes missed) and specificity 90.91%. This small single-center cohort does not support a useful high-sensitivity screen; its test remains frozen. Suspect remains a separate reference category and is not equivalent to diagnosed glaucoma.

## BMRD source-transfer and DINOv2 comparison (exploratory)

The BMRD image-level cleaned set has 1,547 retained labeled images after exact-duplicate/conflicting-label filtering and no patient IDs. Exact-byte SHA-256 matching returned zero shared groups against RFMiD1, FDR26, PAPILA, FIVES, the Bangladesh multi-disease images and ACRIMA; this does not detect recompression or crop relatives. On the source-separated hospital experiments, ConvNeXt-Tiny achieved DR accuracy 69.76% (n=291), balanced accuracy 64.66%, macro-F1 51.76%, macro-AUROC .921; sensitivity for source grade Level 3 was 2.1% (47/48 missed). The AMD result was 53.49% accuracy (n=43), and glaucoma severity was 60.47% accuracy but 40.95% balanced accuracy (n=43). A frozen DINOv2 ViT-B/14 probe improved DR balanced accuracy/F1 to 67.30%/57.88% but reduced accuracy/AUROC to 67.01%/.871; AMD and glaucoma results were mixed. Because the BMRD held-out sites had already been scored by the CNN comparison, DINOv2 is a secondary exploratory comparison, not confirmation or test-selected model choice. All BMRD results are image-level and research-only.

## Perceptual-lineage warning discovered during AMD audit

An independent two-descriptor audit of the Rajbari AMD model's previously reported BMRD score found likely image-lineage overlap missed by exact-byte SHA-256. Of 124 BMRD images, 118 had a Rajbari nearest neighbor at 64-bit DCT pHash distance at most 8 (79 at distance 0); 113 had both pHash and dHash distances at most 8. The reported BMRD 100% result is withdrawn as transfer evidence. This does not prove every pair is the same original, but makes the test unsuitable for an independence claim. Per-image neighbor pairs remain private on the L4.
